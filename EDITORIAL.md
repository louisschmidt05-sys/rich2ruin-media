# Rich 2 Ruin — editorial line

- **Niche:** true stories of money history. Three series, shown as a badge on every reel:
  - **THE CRASH** — bubbles and market crashes
  - **THE SCAM** — financial frauds (only people convicted in court, or long-dead historical figures)
  - **RICH TO RUIN** — fortunes, banks or financiers that rose and collapsed
- **Language:** English. **Voice:** Kokoro `bm_george`, speed 1.06, AI-labelled on every platform.
- **Tone:** rhythmic and punchy. Hook in the first 2 seconds, one big "crash" moment, a twist near the end.
- **Look:** dark background, gold / red / cream, Inter Display + Lora italic, word-by-word captions.
  Alternate archive style (old stories, public-domain images) and graphic style (modern stories).
- **Sound:** generated music (no third-party rights), generated SFX at moderate level.

## Hard rules
1. Every fact verified; disputed figures dropped or qualified. Invented chart shapes labelled "illustrative".
2. Images: public domain or CC0 only (the fetch robot enforces it). No photos of living people unless
   clearly public domain. Never fake portraits; use the generic silhouette (`name` scene) instead.
3. No financial advice, no promotion of any financial product, crypto or broker (French law of 9 June 2023
   on commercial influence applies to the owner).
4. Avoid stories centred on suicide or self-harm, and avoid allegations against people not convicted.
5. Original content only: never repost third-party clips (Instagram originality rules).
